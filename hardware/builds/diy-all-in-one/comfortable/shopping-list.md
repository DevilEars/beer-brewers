## Suggested order of buying

1. Review this list and the open questions in [`spec.md`](spec.md).
2. Re-check live stock, especially the pump, DS18B20 kit, Stellenbosch-only SSR kit, and the RS RCD lead time.
3. Ask Beyond Brewing about the CFC 9.5 mm hook-up before paying for the chiller.
4. Book the electrician **before** intercepting the DigiBoil element.
5. Water-test the DigiBoil on its stock controller first, as in the spec's build phases.
