## Suggested order

1. Get the design reviewed (PCB and mains layout) and book the electrician.
2. Buy the kettle first and measure it. Update the drawings if anything is off by more than 3 mm.
3. Source the 2.2 kW element (ask Beyond Brewing about KegLand KL03506) **before** punching any hole.
4. Get a laser-cut quote for the malt-pipe flat pattern.
5. Order the electronics after the PCB is laid out and reviewed.
